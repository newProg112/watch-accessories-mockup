Care & Details

How do I change a watch strap?

Most watch straps attach via spring bars. We include a spring bar remover with our tool sets. Simply align the tool with the spring bar, push gently, and the bar retracts for easy removal. If you're uncertain, our care guides have step-by-step instructions.

Do your straps fit all watches?

Our straps come in standard widths (18mm, 20mm, 22mm, 24mm). Check your watch specifications to find the right size. We're here to help if you're unsure—reach out and we'll confirm the fit.

How do I care for leather straps?

Avoid prolonged water exposure and store in a cool, dry place. Our leather develops character over time with proper care. The care kit includes everything you need. We also provide detailed care cards with each leather purchase.
